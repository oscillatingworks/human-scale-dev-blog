About this blog

Human Scale is a game about arriving in a new city and building a life there: finding a room, a job and people you like, one street at a time.

This blog follows the game while we make it. A short post comes out on days when something changed that you could see or feel in the city, and a longer one sums up the week on Sundays.

How the posts are written. AI helps us write the posts from the studio’s daily notes, and every post is checked before it goes out. The pictures are screenshots from the game itself.
