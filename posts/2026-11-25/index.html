25 November 2026 · Today in the city

Street signs, hats and green gardens

Today we can tell you where you are. Street names now appear on signs at the corners of junctions.

Names on the corners

Each street gets one sign per corner, and signs never overlap each other. Walk to a junction and look up. You know which street you are on.

Why it matters: on foot you decide at every corner. A sign helps you choose, and you do not need a map. Short blocks give you many corners, so a name on each one is useful.

Hats, scarves and caps

The people on the street are redrawn with more detail, and they are 10% larger. They now wear head coverings and hats.

People dress for the day. The hair also sits under a hat in the right way.

More green around homes

Gardens are now drawn in the city. You see lawns, garden edges with gates, paths and drives. The city also builds estates with long slabs and towers in open green land. Homes there are varied on purpose, so a walk takes you through different kinds of places.

The soft edge where a garden meets the street is a place where people slow down. A gate, a hedge or a short path makes the street friendlier.

A challenge: more people, the same smooth game

Better looking people must not slow the game. So we moved work out of the main loop. The crowd now finds the walking path for a new walker in the background. Street marks and grass edges are also made in the background. The crowd has a time budget for each frame.

We also draw art for the insides of shops. We have a bakery, a grocer, small rooms and large rooms. We do not have these shops open to you yet.
