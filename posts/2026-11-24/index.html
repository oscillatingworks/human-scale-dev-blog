Hello from Human Scale

Hello, and welcome to the Human Scale dev blog.

Human Scale is a top-down pixel-art game about arriving in a new city and building a life there. You find a room, look for a job, meet people and slowly learn the streets. The cities are made for walking: small shops, squares, buses and people with their own day.

This blog follows the game while we make it. On days when something changes that you could see or feel in the city, a short post comes out in the evening. On Sundays, a longer post sums up the week. The picture at the top of each post is always the same street, at the same spot, so you can watch the city change over time.

Today in the city

New art for street props. The bench, the glass bins, the poster column, the post box and the skip get new pixel art. They look richer on the street, and we like them a lot.

Menus follow your mouse. On the start screen, the pause menu and the registration form, you can now point at a button to pick it. The keyboard and a gamepad still work too.

A clear highlight on the phone. Every control on the in-game phone now shows clearly when it is ready to press, in both phone themes.

One challenge. The new props looked better than before, but some were not seen straight from above, and their outlines did not match the rest of the game. We fix both before more of this art goes in.

Next: more of the city gets the same new art, starting with the street props of every city style.
