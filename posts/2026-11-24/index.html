24 November 2026 · Today in the city

Neighbourhoods get their own character

Hello, and welcome. Human Scale is a top-down pixel-art game about arriving in a new city and building a life there: a room, a job, people you meet, one street at a time. The cities are made for walking.

On evenings when something changed, we post a short note here. On Sundays, we write a longer one. The picture at the top is always the same street, so you can watch it change.

Today in the city

Neighbourhoods now have their own character. Until now, a big part of the map was one pattern repeated. Now each district is made of neighbourhoods that feel different: long rows of narrow buildings, a 1960s estate of slabs in open green land, an old warehouse area. The Central European city also gets a workers’ quarter.

A walk now crosses several small worlds. We like that on purpose. When no single kind of building covers most of the city, a street stays interesting at walking speed, and you see something new every few minutes.

A clear outline on the phone. Every control on the in-game phone now shows a clear outline when it is selected, in both the dark and the light look. It is the first step to a phone you can use fully with a keyboard or a gamepad, which we plan next.

New street props. The bench, the glass bins, the poster column, the post box and the skip get new pixel art, made with AI help, so the edge of the street has more to look at. The edge matters. A bench or a café table makes a soft place to stop, and people stay where the edge is soft. A long blank wall makes them walk faster.

One problem: bridges that zigzagged

In some cities, a street crossing a river made a Z. It went over the water, stepped sideways, and came back again. A few even crossed the river twice.

We changed the rule. A street now crosses a river once and in a straight line, and only to the other bank. Bus stops stay off the bridges. We checked the cities again, and the bridges now run straight.

Next, we keep working on how the neighbourhoods look from above.
